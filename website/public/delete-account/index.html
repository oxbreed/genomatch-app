Account

Delete your GenoMatch account

You can delete your account yourself in the app, or ask us to do it by email. You do not need the app installed to request deletion.

In the app

Open GenoMatch and go to Profile → Company & policies → Delete Account. Confirm with your password. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

By email

Send a request from the email address on your account to privacy@genomatch.app with the subject "Delete my account". We verify ownership of the address and complete deletion within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: display name, date of birth, city, genotype, pronouns, bio, interests and lifestyle details
  • Your photos
  • Your matches, likes and passes
  • Your messages
  • Your push notification tokens and session records

What is kept, and for how long

We retain a minimal record of moderation reports, blocks and safety actions where we are required to for the protection of other members, and records we must keep to meet legal or accounting obligations. These are retained no longer than required and are described in the Privacy Policy.
