Help

Support

Email hello@genomatch.app and we will get back to you. For data-protection requests use privacy@genomatch.app.

Delete your account

In the GenoMatch app go to Profile → Delete Account and confirm with your password. That removes your profile, photos, matches, and messages, subject to limited legal retention described in the Privacy Policy.

Reset your password

Use Forgot password in the app. The email link opens GenoMatch when the app is installed. If it does not open, request a new email from Sign In.

Age

GenoMatch is strictly 18+. We remove accounts that appear to belong to minors.

Not medical advice

Genotype on GenoMatch is self-reported. Compatibility copy is educational only and does not replace a clinician or genetic counsellor.
