GenoMatch Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026. Genomatch Ltd Nigeria (RC No. 9236521), a product of Oxbreed Management Ltd, is the data controller.

We collect account details you provide (name, date of birth, email, genotype, profile text), photos you upload, approximate location if you enable it, and basic device diagnostics. We use this to run matching, messaging, safety review, and the product itself.

We do not sell personal data. We share it with infrastructure providers (authentication, database, image hosting) under contract. Genotype is self-reported and treated as sensitive; it is not a laboratory result.

You may edit profile data in the app or request deletion from Profile → Delete Account, or email privacy@genomatch.app.
