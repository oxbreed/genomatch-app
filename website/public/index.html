Dating with genotype on the table, from the start.

GenoMatch is for adults 18 and over. Members share a self-reported sickle cell genotype (AA, AS, SS, AC, SC) so compatibility is part of the conversation before the first date. Educational information only — not a lab test, diagnosis, or medical advice.

Questions, account help, or data requests: hello@genomatch.app. Privacy: privacy@genomatch.app.

How matching works

You declare your genotype when you create an account. GenoMatch shows educational compatibility signals for sickle cell pairings. Confirm anything that matters for your family with a qualified clinician or genetic counsellor.

Safety

Report and block are in the app. Messages and profile text are screened before they are posted. New photos are queued for review. Delete your account any time from Profile → Delete Account.
