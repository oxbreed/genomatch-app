Dating with genotype on the table, from the start.

GenoMatch is a dating app for adults 18 and over. Members may add a self-reported genotype (AA, AS, SS, AC, or SC) to their profile. GenoMatch does not test that information or turn it into a health result.

Questions, account help, or data requests: hello@genomatch.app. Privacy: privacy@genomatch.app.

How matching works

You can enter a genotype when you create an account. Other members see the letters you typed. GenoMatch does not calculate disease risk, give medical advice, or replace a clinician.

Safety

Report and block are in the app. Messages and profile text are screened before they are posted. New photos are queued for review. Delete your account any time from Profile → Delete Account.
